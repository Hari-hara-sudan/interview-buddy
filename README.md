🚀 What is VoxMentor?
VoxMentor is a comprehensive interview preparation platform that combines:

✅ AI-Powered Voice Interviews — Real-time voice-based mock interviews with intelligent feedback using VAPI and advanced AI models (OpenAI, Gemini, Groq)

✅ Resume Assessment — Get detailed analytics and insights on your resume with company-specific optimization recommendations

✅ Live Job Board Integration — Browse real job openings aggregated from major job platforms (Adzuna API) tailored to your skill level

✅ Company-Specific Preparation — Customized interview questions and preparation modules based on target companies

✅ Multi-Skill Assessment — Test your aptitude, programming skills, and technical knowledge in one platform

✅ Personalized Analytics — Track your progress with detailed performance metrics and improvement suggestions

💡 Key Features:
🎙️ Voice-interactive AI interviews with natural conversation flow
📊 Real-time feedback and assessment analytics
🔐 Secure authentication with Firebase
📱 Responsive design for desktop and mobile
🌐 LinkedIn integration for profile-based recommendations
💼 Live job market data and insights
